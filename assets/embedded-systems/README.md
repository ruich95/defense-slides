# Embedded Systems illustrations

## Current slide illustrations

The three transparent PNG illustrations were copied from the approved `../../../figures/embedded-systems-drafts/v6/` on 24 October 2026:

- `braking-system.png`: simplified car with foreground brake pedal.
- `robot-controller.png`: square robot-arm composition with central controller chip and lower support.
- `aircraft-attitude-control.png`: aircraft with rotation arrows.

Created and iteratively edited with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompts and version history are recorded in the draft folders. The slide preserves their proportions and matches their visible subject heights, excluding transparent margins. The build embeds the PNGs as data URLs for offline use.
