Research Question and Objectives

The design process presents two challenges:

  • Automation and correctness throughout the design process.
  • Connecting abstraction levels, despite different representations and tools.

This leads to the research question:

Can a component-based design framework be realised or embedded in

  • a general-purpose functional programming language,
  • which enables both the logical and computational aspects of the meta-language to be leveraged
  • to deliver a formally accountable design process for synchronous systems?
O1: Support stateful, black-box components.
O2: Express models and design steps in Idris2, supporting execution and verification.
O3: Provide accessible modelling through Idris2’s language features and tools.
