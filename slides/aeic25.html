Design Transformations

Two approaches to define design transformations:

  • Abstract interpretations: interpret a SynQ model into a transformed model.
  • Idris2 functions: transform the types and terms used to construct a model.

The balance-tree transformation uses:

  • Well-formed models through SynQ’s typing discipline.
  • Type-aware construction of reducers.
  • Functions that compute types and reshape their associated terms.
