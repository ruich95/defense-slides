Conclusion and Future Work

Conclusion

  • QTT supports disciplined construction of stateful synchronous models.
  • SynQ combines modelling, interpretation and formal reasoning within Idris2.
  • Idris2 functions express design transformations whose stated properties can be proved.
  • Case studies include the rising-pattern detector, FIR filters, an FM receiver and a FIFO buffer.

Future work

  • Extend support to higher abstraction levels.
  • Develop domain-specific specification, design automation and proof automation.
