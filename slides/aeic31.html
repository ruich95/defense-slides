Conclusion and Future Work

Conclusion

  • O1Stateful, black-box components: disciplined composition and explicit behavioural contracts, demonstrated by registers and RV32-I.
  • O2Models, interpretations and transformations in Idris2: detector proof, conditional balancing theorem and product-elimination results.
  • O3Accessible modelling through variables, elaboration and type inference: detector and hierarchical FM examples.

SynQ demonstrates that a component-based framework for synchronous system design can be embedded in Idris2, supporting executable and formally accountable design processes.

Future work

  • Extend support to higher abstraction levels.
  • Develop domain-specific specification, design automation and proof automation.
