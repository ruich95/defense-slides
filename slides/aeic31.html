Conclusion

  • O1Support stateful, black-box components.

    Linear types control their use and composition; behavioural contracts support reasoning about their behaviour.

  • O2Express models and design steps in Idris2, supporting execution and verification.

    Tagless-final embedding represents models as Idris2 terms, design steps are functions, and dependent types support specification and proof.

  • O3Provide accessible modelling through Idris2’s language features and tools.

    Variables, elaboration and type inference help designers express and configure models.

SynQ demonstrates that a component-based framework for synchronous system design can be embedded in Idris2, supporting executable and formally accountable design processes.
