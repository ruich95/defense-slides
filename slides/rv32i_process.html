RV32-I: From Modelling to Implementation

The RV32-I case study demonstrates
the design process.

  • O1Modelling and simulation: stateful, black-box components.
  • Reasoning and validation: proofs, partial simulation and property-based testing.
  • Implementation: manual transformation and mapping to Verilog HDL.

This shallowly embedded DSL does not yet provide sufficient support for the design process.

  • O2Implementation: support systematic mapping to target platforms.
  • Semantics: align behaviour across different interpretations.
  • Refinement and transformation: support their systematic application and verification.
  • O3Modelling: support readable models beyond point-free composition.
